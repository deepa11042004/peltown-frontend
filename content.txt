# FAQ Section

## Is WhatsApp API different from WhatsApp Business App?

Yes. The API is designed for businesses that need automation, integrations, team access, and large-scale messaging capabilities. ([deepclick.com][1])

## Can you integrate WhatsApp with our website?

Yes. We can connect WhatsApp with websites, CRMs, ERPs, e-commerce stores, and custom software.

## Can multiple employees use one WhatsApp number?

Yes. Shared inbox functionality allows multiple team members to manage conversations from the same business number. ([WBIZ.IN][4])

## Can WhatsApp send automated notifications?

Yes. Businesses can send approved template messages for orders, appointments, payments, OTPs, and other notifications. ([WhatsApp for Business][2])

## Is WhatsApp API free?

No. Meta charges based on messaging categories and usage. Additional setup and management costs may apply depending on requirements. ([Reddit][6])
